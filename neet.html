Medical Entrance Focus

The NEET-UG Academic Pathway

The National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET-UG) is the single gateway to undergraduate medical (MBBS), dental (BDS), and AYUSH education across India. It evaluates high-speed accuracy across 720 total marks, requiring complete familiarity with NCERT textbooks alongside crisp numerical dexterity in Physics and Chemistry.

Our two-year Intermediate BiPC curriculum synchronizes the state intermediate syllabus with line-by-line NCERT decoding. Rather than teaching medical entrance as an afterthought, every chapter's descriptive board concepts are accompanied by assertion-reasoning questions, match-the-column drills, and diagram-based questions.

With 360 marks dedicated purely to Biology (Botany & Zoology), students cultivate instant recall techniques, allowing them to conserve time for analytical calculations in Physics.

Curriculum Architecture

Subject-Wise Learning Coverage

Exhaustive mastery across Biology, Physics, and Chemistry tailored to the NEET-UG examination blueprint.

Biology (Botany & Zoology)

360 Marks Focus — 100% NCERT line-by-line coverage with comprehensive biological diagram analysis.

  • Botany: Plant Kingdom, Morphology, Anatomy, Mineral Nutrition, Photosynthesis, Respiration & Plant Growth.
  • Zoology: Animal Diversity, Structural Organisation in Animals & Biomolecules.
  • Human Physiology: Digestion, Breathing, Circulation, Excretion, Locomotion & Neural Control.
  • Genetics & Evolution: Mendelian Inheritance, DNA Replication & Darwinian Mechanisms.
  • Ecology & Biotechnology: Ecosystems, Biodiversity, Genetic Engineering Tools & Applications.

Physics for NEET

180 Marks Focus — Simplifying numerical mechanics, formula linkages, and high-frequency units.

  • Mechanics: Units & Dimensions, Motion in a Straight Line/Plane, Work-Energy & Gravitation.
  • Properties of Matter: Viscosity, Surface Tension & Thermodynamics.
  • Electrodynamics: Electric Charges, Current Electricity, Moving Charges & Alternating Currents.
  • Optics: Lens Maker Formula, Compound Microscopes & Wave Interference.
  • Modern Physics: Dual Nature, Radioactivity, Logic Gates & Semiconductor Diodes.

Chemistry for NEET

180 Marks Focus — Scoring high through NCERT inorganic memorization and high-yield organic named reactions.

  • Physical Chemistry: Solid State, Solutions, Electrochemistry, Surface Chemistry & Equilibrium.
  • Inorganic Chemistry: Periodic Trends, Coordination Compounds, P-Block, D & F-Block Elements.
  • Organic Chemistry: Alkyl Halides, Alcohols & Phenols, Aldehydes & Ketones, Amines & Biomolecules.
Systematic Strategy

NEET Preparation Methodology

A proven cyclical system designed to build accuracy, prevent negative marking, and maximize score retention.

1

NCERT Concept Learning

Sentence-by-sentence analysis of standard NCERT textbooks with detailed marginal notes.

2

Topic-Wise Practice

Immediate post-lecture MCQ sheets containing assertion-reasoning and fact-based queries.

3

Multiple-Choice Drills

Timed speed drills aiming for 45-second average solution speed per Biology question.

4

Chapter-Wise Tests

Testing each unit with 50-question assessments under strict OMR sheet bubbling formats.

5

Full Mock Tests

Complete 200-question (180 to attempt) 3-hour 20-minute national NEET simulations.

6

Revision & Analysis

Error categorization into conceptual confusion, wrong bubbling, or rushed calculations.
