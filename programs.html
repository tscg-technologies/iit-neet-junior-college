Our Academic Programs

Engineered pathways designed to prepare students for both state intermediate board credentials and competitive entrance examinations.

Illustrative Two-Year Program

Intermediate MPC + IIT-JEE Preparation

An integrated dual-focus curriculum engineered for students aspiring to crack JEE Main, JEE Advanced, and state engineering entrance examinations while securing outstanding marks in Intermediate Board Exams.

  • Core Subjects: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, English, and Second Language.
  • Entrance Focus: Full syllabus coverage for JEE Main (CBT) and JEE Advanced.
  • Problem-Solving Regimen: Daily Practice Problem Sheets (DPPs) and level-wise question assignments.
  • Career Pathways: IITs, NITs, IIITs, BITS, and prestigious engineering & technological institutions.

MPC Academic Structure & Coverage

Mathematics Coverage:

Algebra (Matrices, Quadratic, Complex Numbers), Coordinate Geometry, Trigonometry, Vectors & 3D, and full Differential & Integral Calculus.

Physics Coverage:

Mechanics (Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Rotational Dynamics), Thermodynamics, Electrostatics, Magnetism, Optics, and Modern Physics.

Chemistry Coverage:

Physical Chemistry (Stoichiometry, Equilibrium, Electrochemistry), Inorganic (Coordination, P-Block), and Organic Reaction Mechanisms.

Illustrative Two-Year Program

Intermediate BiPC + NEET-UG Preparation

An intensive medical preparatory program integrating Intermediate Board Biology with exhaustive NCERT conceptual drills and competitive problem-solving for NEET-UG.

  • Core Subjects: Botany, Zoology, Physics, Chemistry, English, and Second Language.
  • Entrance Focus: Line-by-line NCERT Biology mastery, numerical Physics, and high-scoring Chemistry.
  • Practical Exposure: Microscopic analysis, specimen identification, and diagram-based assessments.
  • Career Pathways: MBBS, BDS, AYUSH, Veterinary Sciences, Biotechnology, and Allied Medical Careers.
Detailed NEET Curriculum Enquire for BiPC

BiPC Academic Structure & Coverage

Botany Coverage:

Plant Diversity, Plant Anatomy, Plant Physiology (Photosynthesis, Respiration), Genetics, Molecular Basis of Inheritance, and Ecology.

Zoology Coverage:

Animal Kingdom, Structural Organisation, Complete Human Physiology, Human Reproduction, Health & Disease, and Biotechnology Principles.

Physics & Chemistry:

Specially curated numerical problem classes focusing on high-accuracy NEET calculation speeds and memory-driven organic reactions.

Flexible Learning Paths

Additional Academic Options

Specialized batches structured for pure board preparation or dedicated repeaters.

Board Only Stream

Intermediate MPC (Board Focused)

Structured exclusively for students who wish to focus 100% on securing top scores in state intermediate board examinations without entrance coaching load.

  • Complete alignment with prescribed State Board syllabus textbooks.
  • Intensive descriptive theory writing drills and 4-mark / 8-mark question mastery.
  • Complete practical laboratory examination guidance.
Board Only Stream

Intermediate BiPC (Board Focused)

Designed for students targeting high aggregate board percentages for university undergraduate admissions in pure sciences, horticulture, or agricultural degrees.

  • Rigorous diagram practice and descriptive scientific presentation methods.
  • Systematic coverage of Botany & Zoology board blueprints.
  • Fully guided laboratory records and viva voce training.
1-Year Intensive

IIT-JEE Long-Term (Repeater Batch)

Full-time one-year program for Class 12 / Intermediate pass-out students seeking targeted revision, advanced problem drills, and mock test series to maximize JEE rank.

  • Condensed 30-week rapid syllabus coverage.
  • Over 10,000 curated questions and 50+ computer-based mock tests.
  • Dedicated mentor strategy sessions to eliminate negative marking.
1-Year Intensive

NEET-UG Long-Term (Repeater Batch)

Dedicated one-year crash/repeater coaching designed exclusively for medical aspirants who wish to reattempt NEET-UG with refined time-management and NCERT revision.

  • Daily 180-question timed mock drills in NEET simulation format.
  • Intensive Biology line-by-line decoding and error correction.
  • Physics numerical shortcuts and mental calculation workshops.
