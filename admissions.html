Prerequisites

General Eligibility Information

Admission into the two-year Intermediate course requires successful completion of secondary schooling:

  • Successful completion of Class 10 (SSC / CBSE / ICSE or any recognized equivalent state board).
  • Foundational interest in Mathematics & Physical Sciences (for MPC Stream).
  • Foundational interest in Biological Sciences & Physical Chemistry (for BiPC Stream).
  • Readiness to follow disciplined study routines and regular examination schedules.
Regulatory Notice: Final eligibility, age criteria, and certificate verifications are determined by the applicable State Intermediate Board regulations.
Checklist

Documents Required

Prospective students are requested to keep the following credentials prepared during admission counselling:

  • Original and photocopies of Class 10 Marksheet / Passing Certificate.
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) and Conduct Certificate from the last attended school.
  • Government-issued student and parent identity proof (e.g. Aadhaar Card).
  • Recent passport-size colour photographs (6 copies).
  • Migration Certificate (for non-state board or out-of-state applicants).
